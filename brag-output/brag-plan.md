# Толкувач: brag plan

**What it is:** a live meeting interpreter that runs on your own laptop. You speak Macedonian, the others hear French (or one of 21 languages), and their speech comes back to you as Macedonian subtitles.
**For whom:** anyone in a Zoom, Meet or Teams call in a language they don't speak.
**Different:** one local model (SeamlessM4T v2, 4.7 GB VRAM) does speech to speech. You pick a female or male voice, natural (online) or local.
**Hook:** sound. A Macedonian sentence is spoken, then the same sentence comes out in a natural French voice. The viewer hears the product.
**Tone:** default, clean, in the app's own colors (blue for me, green for them).
**No** file paths, people's names, company names or device names on screen. The header crop drops the device names; the voice card is shot without the hint text that names the voice provider.
